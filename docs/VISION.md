# ReadySignal Vision

## Mission

Prepare SDRs to have better conversations by putting the right prospects, reliable contact information, and a clear reason to call in front of them every day.

## Vision

Every salesperson starts the day knowing exactly who to call and why.

## The Problem

SDRs spend too much time:

- Searching databases and CRMs
- Researching companies and contacts
- Sorting through inaccurate contact information
- Deciding who is worth calling
- Doubting whether poor results come from their skills or bad data

This takes time away from learning their business, improving on the phone, and having productive conversations.

## The ReadySignal Experience

An SDR opens ReadySignal and sees a manageable queue of the best prospects to call today.

Each recommendation includes:

- Contact name
- Job title
- Company
- Reliable contact information
- A concise reason to call today
- Relevant notes from past calls, meetings, or company activity

After the call, the SDR quickly records an outcome and an optional note before moving directly to the next prospect.

## Product Principles

1. Prepare people. Do not replace them.
2. Readiness is more important than excessive research.
3. Momentum is more important than exploration.
4. Trust is more important than contact volume.
5. Signals are more useful than raw data.
6. Every call and user response should improve future recommendations.
7. The system should learn from the team while personalizing for each user.
8. If a feature delays the next call, it probably does not belong in the core experience.
9. The homepage should be a queue, not a dashboard.
10. A user should understand who to call next within five seconds.

## Version 1

Version 1 is initially built for Alex to test during his own SDR workflow.

Its first goal is simple:

> Generate a trustworthy daily list of approximately 20–30 prospects, with each contact's name, company, title, contact information, and a one-line reason to call.

Version 1 should remain simple and focused. It will not attempt to become a full CRM, a massive contact database, or a complicated analytics platform.

## Long-Term Direction

ReadySignal should eventually work for SDR teams in any industry.

Each organization will teach the system:

- What it sells
- How its team pitches the product
- Which buyers and job functions matter
- Which companies fit its ideal customer profile
- Which timing signals matter
- What successful calls and meetings look like

ReadySignal will combine this knowledge with public information, connected sales tools, call outcomes, and user feedback to continuously improve its recommendations.
